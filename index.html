Strafe Privacy Policy

App: Strafe (Android, package dev.strafe.surf) · Effective 10 October 2026

In short: Strafe does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

Data we collect

None. Strafe does not collect any information about you or your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not ask for any Android permissions, including network access. The app cannot send data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

To remember your best score and your settings (control scheme, look speed and similar), Strafe saves a small amount of game data inside the app's private storage on your phone. It never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Strafe.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your own Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. Google handles this under your device settings and Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive or have access to these backups.

Sharing

We do not share data with anyone, because we do not have any.

Children

Strafe does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Google Play

Strafe is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you browse, download or update apps in the Play Store. That is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that needs data, the updated policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date before that version is released.
