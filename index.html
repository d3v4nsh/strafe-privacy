Strafe Privacy Policy

App: Strafe (Android, package dev.strafe.surf) · Effective 10 October 2026 · Updated 10 October 2026

In short: Strafe does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. The Android app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. The web version has no ads, analytics or tracking; if you choose to join its global leaderboard, it stores the name you pick and your best distances.

Data we collect

None. Strafe does not collect any information about you or your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not ask for any Android permissions, including network access. The app cannot send data anywhere.

Data stored on your device

To remember your best score and your settings (control scheme, look speed and similar), Strafe saves a small amount of game data inside the app's private storage on your phone. It never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Strafe.

Web version

Strafe can also be played in a web browser at strafe.classopedia.co.in. It has no ads, analytics or tracking. Your settings and personal bests are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. The game files are served by our hosting provider, Vercel, which like any web server processes your IP address and basic request information to deliver the page and protect against abuse, under Vercel's Privacy Policy. After the first visit the game can run offline.

Global leaderboard (web version only)

The leaderboard is optional. If you pick a name for it:

  • What we store: the name you choose, a random player ID, and your best distance on each map style. Your name and distances are shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Cookie: we set one cookie, strafe_id, holding your random player ID, so the game knows the scores are yours. It is needed for the leaderboard to work, is not used for tracking or advertising, and expires after a year. Without a name, no cookie is set.
  • Abuse limits: to limit spam, we count requests per network using a one-way hash of your IP address. These counters are deleted automatically within a day; your IP address itself is not stored.
  • Where: this data is stored with Upstash (a database provider used through Vercel) and is not shared with anyone else or used for anything but the leaderboard.
  • Deleting it: email us your leaderboard name and we will remove it and your scores. Clearing your browser's cookies disconnects you from that name.

The Android app does not include the leaderboard and does not connect to the internet.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your own Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. Google handles this under your device settings and Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive or have access to these backups.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with anyone. Leaderboard names and distances are public by design, as described above.

Children

Strafe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Leaderboard names should not be real names; if a child has posted one, email us and we will remove it.

Google Play

Strafe is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you browse, download or update apps in the Play Store. That is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that needs data, the updated policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date before that version is released.
